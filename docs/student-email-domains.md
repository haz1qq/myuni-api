## Notes

- Separate current Google and Microsoft address suffixes are retained as separate array entries.
- UTeM's general `@utem.edu.my` suffix is used for staff; the official student suffix is
  `@student.utem.edu.my`.
- UTHM's older `@siswa.uthm.edu.my` suffix is legacy; the current official student suffix is
  `@student.uthm.edu.my`.
- UM's temporary `@siswa-old.um.edu.my` migration mailbox is not included.

## IPTS notes

- `@student.unitar.my` is retained only for UNITAR International University. KLMUC remains `null`
  because the official UNITAR example is not KLMUC-specific.
- INTI colleges and UCSI entities were kept `null`: official pages confirm students receive an
  email account but do not state the address suffix (INTI's `*.student.newinti.edu.my` portal
  hostname is not treated as mailbox-suffix evidence).
- UOW Malaysia KDU College kept `null`: no KDU-specific evidence was found; the documented
  `@student.uow.edu.my` format is for UOW Malaysia itself.
